- Your bookmarks, history, passwords, notes and settings are stored on this PC, encrypted where

  translation (Google Translate), the AI assistant (Anthropic or Groq, with your own key),

- Sync writes end-to-end encrypted files to a folder you choose (for example in OneDrive).
